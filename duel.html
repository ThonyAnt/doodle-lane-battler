  • Draw two things before each round: a sword, a bow, a bomb, a shield, wings, a snack. You share 100 ink between them, and more ink means a stronger item.
  • Fight live: move, double jump, and use your two drawings. An empty slot is a punch. First to 2 rounds wins.
  • How to trigger each move (A and B are your two drawings; F and G on a keyboard, K and L for the second player):
    · Combo: stand still and tap the button again and again. Swords, spears and hammers chain 3 moves, fists chain 4.
    · Running attack: hold a direction until you are sprinting, then press.
    · Air attack: jump, then press.
    · Special: hold down (S, ↓ or ▼) and press. Melee weapons launch the enemy upward (the hammer spins); bows, guns, staffs and throwing stars fire a slow charged shot.
    · Rapid fire: with a ranged item, keep tapping after the first shot.
    Each combo move and shot has two animations picked at random. Practice mode has no clock and a dummy that never dies, and lets you swap items at any time.
  • Fighting styles: a sword, a spear, a hammer or gloves each fight differently, and so do a bow, a gun, a magic staff or something you throw. Tap for a combo; attack while running, in the air, or holding down for other moves.
  • fire burns after the hit · water slows and shoves · plant heals you for part of the damage · plain ink hits 15% harder.
  • Shields only block from the front. Your own bombs hurt you too. Snacks heal 3 times per round.
