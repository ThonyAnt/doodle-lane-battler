How a drawing becomes a unit

  • Ink is the price. Every stroke spends ink, and the ink you spend sets the unit's total strength.
  • The reader picks the kind, never the power: ground, flying or stationary; melee, ranged, area or no attack; one element.
  • fire beats plant, plant beats water, water beats fire (1.5x damage).
  • Flying units cannot be hit by ground melee. Ranged and area attacks reach them.
  • Stationary drawings (a wall, a tower) hold a spot in front of your base and are tougher.
  • Last 30 seconds: ink refills twice as fast. Most base health at 0:00 wins. If the bases are tied, the side that pushed further down the lane wins.
